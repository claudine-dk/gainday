Have questions? We've got answers!

What is GAIN?

GAIN stands for Grace. Acceleration. Increase. Now. It is our church‑wide campaign to rebuild our primary worship home following Hurricane Beryl and prepare for the next chapter of ministry. More than a fundraising initiative, GAIN is a season of faith, generosity, and spiritual growth as we believe in God for collective exponential impact.

How does the two-year pledge work?

A pledge is your personal commitment to give toward the GAIN campaign over a two-year period. Rather than making one gift all at once, you can prayerfully determine an amount and fulfill your commitment over time in the way that works best for you.

Where does my gift go?

Every gift directly supports the restoration of our permanent home and the ministries it makes possible, including:

  • A revitalized worship center where our church family can gather under one roof.
  • Expanded children's and youth environments.
  • Dedicated spaces for counseling, discipleship, and care.
  • Community outreach, food distribution, and crisis support.

Your generosity helps create a place where lives will continue to be transformed for generations to come.

Can I give in installments?

Yes! Most people will fulfill their pledge through recurring monthly, quarterly, or annual gifts over the two-year campaign. You are welcome to give in the way that best fits your financial circumstances.

Who do I contact about a major gift?

If you're interested in making a major gift, learning about naming opportunities, or discussing other ways to partner with the campaign, we'd love to connect with you personally.

Contact Ivy McGregor, Chief Strategy Officer, at iMcGregor@TLHC.org or 713‑931‑8401.

Can I give in ways other than cash?

Yes. While many people choose to fulfill their pledge through financial gifts, there are other meaningful ways to support the GAIN campaign. Depending on your circumstances, you may be able to contribute through gifts such as:

  • Appreciated stocks or securities
  • Real estate
  • Business interests
  • Jewelry or other valuable assets
  • Donor‑advised funds (DAFs)
  • Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs) from eligible retirement accounts
  • Planned or legacy gifts

If you're considering a non‑cash gift, our team is available to help guide you through the process and answer any questions. Contact Ivy McGregor at iMcGregor@TLHC.org.

What if I can't make a large financial commitment?

Every act of generosity matters. We believe GAIN is about faithful stewardship. We encourage every person to pray, seek God's direction, spread the vision, and participate in whatever way He leads. Together, every gift becomes part of the legacy we're building.
