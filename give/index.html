FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Do I have to make a pledge to give?

No. You can give a one-time gift right now without pledging. If you'd like to make a pledge first, visit the Pledge page and it will walk you through it.

How do I give toward my pledge?

Use the form above. Type in the amount you want to give. Then choose One time to give a single amount, or Recurring to give automatically every week, every month, or every year. Every gift you make here is counted toward your pledge. You don't need to do anything extra.

Can I give a little at a time?

Yes. Most people do. Choose Recurring on the form and pick how often: weekly, monthly, or yearly. For example, a $1,200 pledge could be $50 a month for two years, or about $12 a week. You can change or stop a recurring gift at any time.

Where does my money go?

Every dollar goes to rebuilding The Lighthouse Church: the sanctuary, children's and youth rooms, counseling and care spaces, and room to keep serving Houston families. Nothing goes anywhere else.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. If you give online, you'll get an automatic confirmation from the giving platform right away. If you give cash or cheque in person, your gift will appear on your annual giving statement.

Can I give something other than money?

Yes. Some people give stocks, property, or gifts from a retirement account or donor-advised fund. These can be a smart way to give. Email our team at gainjourney@tlhc.org and we'll help you through it.

Who do I talk to if I have a question?

Email gainjourney@tlhc.org or call 713-931-8401. You can also ask anyone at the Welcome Desk on Sunday.

Considering a major gift?

Custom naming opportunities are available for major gifts. Our team would be honoured to talk with you. Email gainjourney@tlhc.org or call 713-931-8401.
