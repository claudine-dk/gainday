Your GAIN journey

Before we move together,
we seek God together

GAIN begins with a personal invitation to draw closer to God and consider what He wants to do in your life during this season.

As a church family, we're setting aside 21 days, October 4–25, for prayer and fasting to quiet the distractions around us, seek God with greater intention, and listen for how He is calling each of us to respond. Everyone fasts together for the full 21 days — choose the level that fits where you are.

  1. 01

    Step In

    Fast from social media.

  2. 02

    Deeper

    Fast from lunch.

  3. 03

    All In

    Fast from food from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Whether you've never fasted before or fasting is already part of your spiritual practice, start where you are.
