The goal

A home for
the next generation

In 2024, Hurricane Beryl took our sanctuary. Together, we're building it again.

We're raising $5 million upfront toward a $12 million rebuild: a worship home for our whole church family, spaces where children learn about Jesus, and room for counseling, food, and care for Houston families.
