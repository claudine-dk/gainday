Our story

Seventeen seasons
of faith and resilience.

Serving Houston, and beyond, since 2009.

  1. 2009

    Five people and a hotel meeting room

    The Lighthouse Church begins with five members in a Holiday Inn meeting room.

  2. 2009/14

    On the road

    Services move through Humble Middle, Kingwood High, and Summer Creek. No building. No pause.

  3. 2015

    A permanent home

    First service at 6650 Rankin Road. Six years after the hotel room, the church has an address.

  4. 2018/22

    Four campuses and a nation

    Campus launches, online broadcasts, and the birth of Lighthouse Nation.

  5. Now

    Nearly one million weekly

    Membership beyond twenty thousand, with a global online community gathering every week.

The trial

In 2024, Hurricane Beryl caused catastrophic damage.

The ceilings came down. The sanctuary was no longer safe. The church did not scatter.

“The hurricane took our facility. It did not take our calling.”

Tested, but not broken. Displaced, but not defeated.

The challenge

What the storm
actually cost.

  • Primary facility significantly damaged.
  • Congregation displaced to multiple temporary locations.
  • Vital community programs paused or eliminated.
  • Ministry momentum disrupted.
  • Rising temporary facility costs draining resources.

Questions

Before you commit.

What is GAIN Day?

GAIN stands for Grace, Acceleration, Increase, Now. It is the campaign to rebuild The Lighthouse Church after Hurricane Beryl. The church‑wide day is Sunday, October 25, 2026.

How does the two-year pledge work?

You commit a total amount and give it across twenty‑four months in whatever rhythm fits your household, whether that is monthly, quarterly, or annually. Your commitment card records the total. You decide the pace.

Where does my gift go?

Toward the $5,000,000 needed upfront on a $12,000,000 rebuild: a revitalized worship home, expanded children and youth facilities, classrooms and counseling suites, and space for food distribution and crisis relief.

Can I give in installments?

Yes. That is exactly what the two-year pledge is designed for. Nothing is due in a single payment.

Who do I contact about a major gift?

Custom naming opportunities are available for major gifts. Reach the campaign team through the church office, or visit tlhc.org.
