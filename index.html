FAQ

Questions? We've
got answers.

What is GAIN?

GAIN stands for Grace. Acceleration. Increase. Now. It's our two-year spiritual and financial campaign to rebuild The Lighthouse Church after Hurricane Beryl destroyed our sanctuary in 2024. The total rebuilding cost is approximately $12 million, with an initial goal of raising $5 million upfront through the generosity and commitment of our church family and supporters.

What is a pledge?

A pledge is a promise to give a certain amount of money to GAIN over the next two years. You pick the amount. You don't pay it all at once. For example, if you pledge $2,400, you could give $100 a month for 24 months. If you pledge $600, that's $25 a month. You can give weekly, monthly, yearly, or whenever you're able. There is no minimum pledge.

How do I give?

Visit the Give page, where you can enter an amount and give using your card or bank account. You can make a one-time gift or set up automatic weekly or monthly giving. If you've already made a pledge, your gifts will count toward your commitment. You can also give in person on Sundays: write "GAIN" on your giving envelope, or choose GAIN when you give electronically in service.

Do I have to make a pledge to give?

No! You can make a one-time gift at any time without making a pledge. Pledges help us plan for the next two years by giving us a better understanding of the financial commitments toward our rebuilding goal. Every gift makes a difference, with or without a pledge.

Where does my money go?

Every dollar given to GAIN goes toward rebuilding our church, including a new sanctuary where we can worship together, classrooms for our children and youth, spaces for counseling and care, and the ability to continue serving Houston families through food and community support. All GAIN contributions are dedicated to our rebuilding efforts.

Is there a minimum amount?

No! There is no minimum pledge or gift. Whether it's $5, $50, or $5,000, every gift makes a difference. The giving chart on the Pledge page provides examples, but you're welcome to give any amount.

What if my situation changes and I can't finish my pledge?

That's totally okay! A pledge is a plan, not a bill. If your circumstances change, you can adjust your pledge, give less, or pause your giving. Simply email gainjourney@tlhc.org and we'll help update your commitment. You won't be charged for any portion of your pledge you're unable to fulfill.

How long does GAIN last?

GAIN is a two-year campaign that began on Commitment Sunday, October 25, 2026, and runs through October 2028. You can make a pledge or give at any time throughout the two-year campaign.
