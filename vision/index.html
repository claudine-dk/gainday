The vision

Get ready
for GAIN

The next chapter will be greater than the last.

GAIN is a declaration of faith. A spiritual journey rooted in the belief that what God wants to accomplish through us begins with the work He does within us.

Whether through prayer, generosity, service, or another step in faith, each of us has a part to play. And when those individual commitments come together, our collective acts of obedience have the power to impact generations.

More than a building

We're building for what happens inside these walls and reaches far beyond them.

  • Our entire church family will once again gather under one roof in a revitalized worship home designed for the future of our ministry.

  • Every Sunday, hundreds more children will learn about Jesus in environments built specifically for them.

  • People in every season of life will find guidance and hope through dedicated classrooms, offices, and counseling suites.

  • Thousands of Houston families will receive food, counseling, and hope through this building every year.

The true legacy of GAIN will be written in the lives of the child who discovers their faith, the family that finds support when they need it most, the person who makes their way back to God, and the communities strengthened because The Lighthouse Church was there to serve.

That's the story we have the opportunity to write together.
