GAIN Stewardship Campaign

Make my pledge

A pledge is a promise. It's you saying, 'Here's what I plan to give over the next two years.' You choose the amount. Then you give toward it bit by bit, every week, every month, or whenever you can. No amount is too small.

How it works

  1. Pray

    Ask God what He wants you to give.

  2. Pledge

    Your two-year commitment using the button below.

  3. Participate

    By giving a one-time gift today, or give toward your two-year pledge.
